ABOUT THE VIDEOGRAPHER

The person behind the perspective.

We're By Design Studio, a creative video and visual storytelling studio that believes the strongest visuals do more than document a moment. They help people feel, remember, and connect with a story.

With a background in cinematography and post-production editing, my approach centers on natural light, intentional pacing, and emotional clarity. Whether working on a boutique brand film, an automotive commercial, or a personal documentary, I prioritize the human core over empty visual noise.

WORKING PROCESS / HOW IT WORKS

A Simple Process. A Thoughtful Result.

A clear, collaborative process that takes a project from its initial idea to the final delivery.

STAGE 01

Discovery

Aligning on core objectives, visual tone, emotional themes, reference moodboards, and delivery requirements.

STAGE 02

Pre-Production

Storyboarding, location scouting, talent casting, camera lens package selection, and shot list coordination.

STAGE 03

Production

On-set directing, camera operation, audio capture, and technical supervision under precise lighting conditions.

STAGE 04

Post-Production

Pacing assembly, sound design mixing, color grading in DaVinci Resolve, title motion graphics, and feedback rounds.

STAGE 05

Delivery

Final ProRes 4444 and H.265 master exports tailored for cinema, broadcast, and high-bitrate social feeds.
